# Boosting

Dusa Boosting adds vehicle boosting contracts on a tablet: contract classes from D to S+, groups, tracker hacks, VIN scratching, a Boost Coin store, a player contract market and signature S+ vehicles. Works on ESX, QBCore and Qbox through Dusa Bridge.
